Snapshot from your order-confirmation, shipping, and delivery emails — pulled and reconciled from Gmail as of .
Brand tabs are generated from each order's category — a brand ordered from more than once gets its own tab instead of falling into "Other".
"Delayed", "Stick %" and "Spent" are computed here, not pulled directly from an email: delayed = no shipping/delivery update in 14+ days since the order date; stick % = share of orders that weren't cancelled; spent = net of cancellations and refunds.
This is a point-in-time snapshot, not a live feed — ask to have it refreshed with the latest order emails whenever you want an update.
PAS is the flat $ cut you pay the person whose card/account/address placed the order — retailers never mention it, so no email sync could ever fill it in. Set it per order, or use PAS/EA — mass apply on the Items table to set a per-unit rate once and push it to every single-item order for that SKU at once (qty × rate). Mixed-item orders are left alone — set those by hand. True cost (checkout total + PAS) updates immediately either way, and everything you enter is saved so it's still there next time you open this.
Order TOTAL already includes tax and shipping — the exact amount the retailer charged. The Items table's PRICE/EA and TOTAL go a step further: each item's price has that order's tax rate (tax ÷ subtotal) applied, plus an even split of that order's shipping/delivery fee across every unit in the order — a $9 shipping charge on a 2-item order adds $4.50 to each item, or $1.80 each on a 5-item order.
Edit / delete: use ✎ to correct any field on an order (status, total, dates, items, notes...) or 🗑 to remove a row entirely — duplicates, test orders, anything that shouldn't be tracked. Both are saved, and every stat and total on this page recalculates from your edited data, not the original pull.
Locations groups every shipping address down to its real house number + street, ignoring apartment/unit/floor/door text and city/zip drift — so all the jig variants of one address collapse into a single row instead of showing up as 30+ separate "different" addresses. Use the address dropdown in the orders filters to jump straight to everything sent to one location.
